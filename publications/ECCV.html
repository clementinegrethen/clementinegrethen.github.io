Abstract

Large geometric foundation models (e.g. MASt3R, 688.6M parameters) achieve state-of-the-art 3D reconstruction from stereo pairs but are prohibitive to deploy on resource-constrained platforms — particularly relevant for lunar space missions. We propose a distillation framework combining (i) SVD-based decoder initialization from the teacher (Eckart–Young optimal), (ii) a feature-alignment loss at intermediate ViT layers, and (iii) full encoder fine-tuning.

Extension to other domains

While the main paper focuses on lunar imagery, our framework transfers to general-purpose datasets without architectural changes — we distill the publicly released MASt3R checkpoint (no domain adaptation) and apply the same recipe.

BlendedMVS (Scenario B)

Distilled S2 ViT-S/14 trained on 30K BlendedMVS pairs (50 epochs). Each row shows: input image · GT depth · our prediction · absolute error.

DTU (cross-domain generalization)

The same distilled student, evaluated on DTU (multi-view stereo) without any retraining. Demonstrates that the geometric features learned from MASt3R generalize across visual domains.

Key insight: a single student trained with our framework on BlendedMVS achieves competitive results on DTU (abs_rel ≈ 7%) without any fine-tuning — confirming that the SVD+Lfeat recipe transfers beyond the lunar domain.
